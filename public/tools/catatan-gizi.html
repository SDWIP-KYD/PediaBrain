💉 Nutrisi Parenteral (TPN/NP)

Parenteral Nutrition
📋 Komposisi Nutrisi Parenteral
  • Protein: (1–2) × BB × 1/22 × 4 = 10–20%
  • Lipid: (1–3) × BB × 9 = 20–30%
  • Karbohidrat: 100 – Protein – Lipid = 50–60%
  • Energi NP: Total kalori – kalori Schofield > 60%
  • Balance N: 150–250 mg N/kgBB/hari
GIR = 0.167 × Dextrose% × Rate (ml/jam) ÷ BB (kg)
Target GIR: 4–6 mg/kgBB/menit
Maks tanpa CVC: 12.5% dextrose

📐 Formula & Rumus Klinis

Quick Reference
⚡ Rumus Kebutuhan Kalori
  • Normal: BBI × RDA = kebutuhan kkal/hari
  • Gizi Buruk (WHO): BBA × 80–220 kkal/kg/hr
  • Obesitas: BBI × RDA – 30%

Rumus BBI per Usia

Bayi: BBI = (usia bln + 9) / 2
1–6 thn: BBI = (usia thn × 2) + 8
7–12 thn: BBI = (usia thn × 7 – 5) / 2
⚠️ Obesitas: Target penurunan BB 500 g/bulan (≈ 2 kg/4 bulan)
